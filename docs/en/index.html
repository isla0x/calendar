Yeobaek

A calendar where you type one line and see your free time at a glance

About the app

Yeobaek reads the events from your iPhone's built-in Calendar and shows them in month, week and day views, and works out the free time between events. Type one line like "tomorrow 3pm dentist 30 min" and it reads the date and time for you and creates the event.

FAQ

Where are my events stored?
In your iPhone's built-in Calendar. Yeobaek does not copy them or upload them to any server.

Can I see other calendars (Google, etc.)?
Yes. Every calendar added to your iPhone under Settings > Calendar > Accounts is shown.

Can I make recurring events or alerts?
Tap "Edit" on an event's detail screen to open the iPhone Calendar editor, where you can set repeats and alerts.

I denied calendar access.
Allow it under Settings > Yeobaek > Calendars. You can also tap "Look around with a sample first" on the first screen to browse without permission.
