Yeobaek

A calendar where you type one line and see your free time at a glance

한국어 · English · Français · 中文 · 日本語

About the app

Yeobaek reads the events from your iPhone's built-in Calendar and shows them in month, week and day views, and works out the free time between events. Type one line like "tomorrow 3pm dentist 30 min #health" and it reads the date, time, length and tag for you and creates the event.

FAQ

Where are my events stored?
In your iPhone's built-in Calendar. Yeobaek does not copy them or upload them to any server. Syncing with iCloud, Google and other accounts is handled by the iPhone Calendar.

Can I see other calendars (Google, etc.)?
Yes. Every calendar added under Settings > Calendar > Accounts is shown, including holiday calendars.

How do I write the one-line input?
Try "tomorrow 3pm dentist 30 min #health", "Friday 7pm dinner" or "2026.11.2~2026.12.18 New project #work". Yeobaek reads the date, time, length, place and tags, and shows you how it read them right below the field. You can write in Korean, English, French, Chinese or Japanese.

How is "free time" calculated?
It finds the gaps between your events during the day. Events with travel time also count the trip there and back as busy time. Tap a free slot to add an event right at that time.

How do projects work?
Turn a long piece of work (say, a project that runs for weeks) into a project. It shows as a bar on the calendar, with a progress bar, a D-day and checkpoints. Events you put inside a project are hidden from the calendar list; tap the project to see that day's events inside it. In the day view they sit as cards inside the project block. They still count toward your free time.

How do to-dos get onto my calendar?
Add a to-do and how long it takes. Yeobaek finds a slot in your free time and first shows it as a "candidate". Add it to your calendar if you like it, and you can undo it all at once afterwards.

Are there widgets?
Yes. You can put widgets for today's remaining free time, a day timeline, projects and to-dos on your Home Screen and Lock Screen. Widget data stays on your device.

What does Pro unlock?
Pro is a one-time purchase (non-consumable) that unlocks Backup & Restore. It saves your colors, projects, to-dos and travel times to a file, and brings them back on a new phone with one "Import". Events themselves live in your device Calendar (iCloud), so they are not part of the backup. If you already bought it, tap "Restore purchase".

I denied calendar access.
Allow it under Settings > Yeobaek > Calendars. You can also tap "Look around with a sample first" on the first screen to browse without permission.

I want to delete my data.
Delete events in the iPhone Calendar. The values Yeobaek keeps itself (colors, projects, to-dos) are removed when you delete the app.
