Privacy Policy

Yeobaek · Effective October 26, 2026

한국어 · English · Français · 中文 · 日本語

In one sentence

Yeobaek does not collect any personal information or send it to a server. Everything is processed on your iPhone.

Information we collect

None. There is no sign-up or login, and no advertising or analytics tools (SDKs). We do not track you.

Calendar access

Yeobaek asks for Calendar access to show the events in your iPhone Calendar (title, date and time, location, notes) and to add, edit or delete events when you ask. This information is used only on your device and is not sent anywhere or stored separately. You can change the permission any time under Settings > Yeobaek > Calendars.

Values kept on your device

Values you set inside Yeobaek, such as event colors, projects, to-dos and travel times, are stored only on your device. Widgets read these values on the same device and do not send them anywhere.

Backup & Restore (Pro)

Backup is a file you create and keep yourself. You decide where to save or send it. Yeobaek does not upload or receive it for you.

Purchases

Pro is a one-time in-app purchase. Apple processes the payment. Yeobaek does not receive or store card details or other payment information.

Network use

Yeobaek does not send or receive your data over the internet. The font used in the app (IBM Plex Sans KR, SIL OFL 1.1) is included in the app.

Sharing an event

"Share" on an event only copies its details to your clipboard. You decide where to send it.

Children's privacy

Yeobaek collects no personal information, so it collects none from children either.

When this policy changes

If it changes, we will post the new date on this page.
