Privacy Policy

Yeobaek · Effective October 25, 2026

In one line

Yeobaek does not collect any personal information or send it to any server. Everything is processed only on your iPhone.

Information we collect

None. There is no sign-up or login, and no advertising or analytics SDK.

Calendar access

Yeobaek asks for Calendar access to show the events on your iPhone (title, date and time, location, notes) and to add, edit or delete events when you ask. This information is used only on your device and is not sent anywhere or stored separately. You can change the permission any time under Settings > Yeobaek > Calendars.

Network use

Yeobaek does not exchange data over the internet. The font used in the app (IBM Plex Sans KR, SIL OFL 1.1) is included in the app.

Sharing events

"Share" in the event details only copies the event text to the clipboard. You decide where to send it.

Children's privacy

Yeobaek collects no personal information, so it collects none from children either.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will post the new date on this page.
